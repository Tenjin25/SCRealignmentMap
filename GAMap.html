🔍 Research Findings

Henry County - Suburban Surge

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+35.66 → 2024: D+29.66

Demographic Shift: Once reliably Republican, now a Democratic stronghold due to rapid suburban growth and increasing racial diversity, particularly among younger black voters.

Newton County - Diversifying Electorate

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+24.12 → 2024: D+15.15

Demographic Shift: Suburban Atlanta county with significant growth in Black and Hispanic populations, shifting from Republican to Democratic in recent cycles.

Cobb County - Urban Professional Influx and Realignment

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+23.11 → 2024: D+14.88

Demographic Shift: Historically a GOP bastion, now solidly Democratic. Driven by an influx of younger, more diverse residents and urban professionals, as well as the presence of Kennesaw State University continuing the college realignment seen around the country.

Douglas County - Black Middle Class Growth

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+25.10 → 2024: D+31.45

Demographic Shift: Suburban county west of Atlanta, with a growing Black middle class and increasing Democratic margins.

Gwinnett County - Rapidly Growing Engine of Diversity

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+31.78 → 2024: D+16.51

Demographic Shift: One of the most diverse counties in the Southeast and second most populous in Georgia. Shifted from Republican to Democratic as Asian, Black, and Hispanic populations have surged.

Jackson County (I-85 corridor) - Exurban Stronghold

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+38.46 → 2024: R+55.09

Demographic Shift: The county is rapidly growing as part of Metro Atlanta's expanding exurbs, with a predominantly white population that remains strongly Republican and has actually increased its Republican lean in recent years. This is a prime example of how growth can reinforce or increase the partisan lean of a county instead of diluting it.

Fayette County - On the Cusp

Competitive Trend: 2000: R+41.31 → 2024: R+3.16

Demographic Shift: Traditionally Republican, but demographic change and proximity to Atlanta make it a likely candidate to flip Democratic in the very near future. Georgia as a whole is now about 33% Black, accelerating these trends.
